## 1. EXECUTIVE VISION

> *"In Hearthbound Hollow, every villager has memories they wish they could sell. You are the only one who can buy."*

**Hearthbound Hollow** is a **single-player cozy narrative simulation** set in a small autumnal village where memories are a **physical, tradable commodity** — captured inside glowing glass orbs. You inherit *The Hollow*, a memory-brokerage shop abandoned by its mysterious previous keeper.

You are not a farmer. You are not a warrior. You are a **memory-keeper** — the most intimate profession a village could have. Villagers come to you to let go of pain, to reclaim what they've lost, to understand what they lived through, or to find something they never experienced. You hold their most vulnerable truths, and what you do with them defines who you become.

### The Studio's North Star

*Hearthbound Hollow* is designed to be the **emotional cozy game of its generation** — a game that lives in players' memories the way *Spiritfarer* did, but with a fundamentally novel core mechanic that no other game has ever executed. Five years from now, **"memory-broker simulation"** should be a recognized subgenre that *we created*.

### Inspirational Comp Set

| Inspiration | What we take from it |
|---|---|
| **Stardew Valley** | The warmth, the daily loop rhythm, the sense of belonging |
| **Spiritfarer** | Emotional weight in a cozy register, making players cry on a bus |
| **Strange Horticulture** | Tactile shop investigation, deduction without stakes |
| **Disco Elysium** | Respect for dialogue, every line matters, moral complexity |
| **Coffee Talk** | Cozy narrative, listening as gameplay |
| **A Short Hike** | Tight, tight, beloved — scope discipline |

**In one pitch line:** *Stardew's warmth × Spiritfarer's heart × Strange Horticulture's puzzles × Disco Elysium's writing — at cozy scale.*

## 4. CORE FANTASY & UNIQUE SELLING POINTS

### The Core Fantasy
You are the **memory-keeper of a village** — the most trusted, most powerful, and most morally complex role imaginable. People give you the most vulnerable pieces of themselves. What you do with those pieces **defines the village's future.**

### USP 1: Memories as a Physical Commodity
Memory orbs are tangible objects you hold, polish, shelve, sort, and sometimes drop. The tactility of memory — the weight of grief, the fragility of a happy birthday, the heat of an old shame — is never metaphorical. It is **in your hands.**

This mechanic has never been executed at this depth in any game. It is *the* defining differentiator.

### USP 2: A Cozy Game With Real Moral Weight
Every memory transaction is a choice with consequences that ripple across seasons. You are not farming crops with no stakes. You are choosing which traumas a widow lives with, which memories a lonely child buys, which village secrets stay buried.

The choices *don't punish* — they *shape.*

### USP 3: 30+ Hand-Authored Villager Memory Maps
Every villager has 12–20 fully-authored memories that together reconstruct their complete life story. Cross-referencing memories across villagers reveals connections the village tried to hide. No filler dialogue. Every line could be in a novel.

### USP 4: Memory Dreams — Infinite Free Content
Every memory in your collection becomes a branching, fully-illustrated narrative vignette when you sleep. Your shelves are tomorrow's dreams. This is the perpetual content engine: **new memories = new dreams = new nights.**

### USP 5: Cozy Framing, Deep Substance
The lighting is warm. The music is gentle. The pace never rushes. But the conversations can break your heart. *Hearthbound Hollow* trusts the player to hold both at the same time.

### USP 6: The Predecessor Mystery
Who was the previous Hollow-keeper? Why did they leave? What did they know? This grand mystery is seeded from frame one (the Cold Open cinematic) and unfolds through every memory, echo, and hidden letter — drawing players forward across the full game arc.

### USP 7: Full Arabic + English Localization
Built from the ground up with a live EN/Arabic language toggle, RTL text support, shaped Arabic rendering, and a dedicated Arabic voice generation pipeline — making this a truly bilingual cozy experience.

## 7. THE DAILY LOOP

The daily loop is the **spine of the game** — the rhythm that makes Hearthbound Hollow a place to return to, not a corridor to walk once.

### The 6-Beat Day

```
1. MORNING AGENDA [J]
   Wake to a parchment card: today's visitors, garden state, almanac headline

2. OPEN FOR BUSINESS [B] — Request Board
   3–4 villager tiles: Keep / Listen / Not-today
   Choose who you help and in what order

3. MEMORY WORK [K] — Living Workbench
   Polish · Cleanse · Sort · Steep
   Varied, gently-masterable. Perfect = Keeper's Hand.

4. VILLAGE WALK — Garden [G] + Hollow [U] + Wall [M]
   Harvest herbs → brew teas → earn coin → buy upgrades
   Connect memories → complete Echo threads

5. EVENING LEDGER
   Review transactions. Day-in-verse prose recap.
   "Before you rest" growth coda if anything advanced.

6. MEMORY DREAM + GOODNIGHT CARD
   Illustrated vignette from your collection.
   Goodnight parchment + Pickle sign-off.
   → tomorrow begins compounded on today.
```

### The Compounding Loop

```
Coin → Hollow upgrades → larger shop capacity
     → new herb garden beds → more tea varieties
     → unlock deeper memory operations
     → access more villager arcs
     → more memories on the Wall
     → more Echo threads to complete
     → more coin rewards
     (loops back)
```

## 8. THE 5 PILLAR MECHANICS

### 8.1 Memory Orbs — The Tactile Core

Memory orbs are the physical form of villagers' memories. Each has:

| Property | Meaning | Game Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Color** | Emotional tone | Joy = warm gold, Grief = deep blue, Shame = murky green, Awe = violet, Longing = amber, Dread = dark red |
| **Clarity** | How intact the memory is | High clarity = vivid; low = faded and dreamy |
| **Cracks** | Trauma markers | Each crack is a wound; cleansing removes some — too many removed erases the truth |
| **Weight** | Significance to the giver | Heavy orbs glow warmer; light ones flicker |
| **Size** | Scale of the memory | A childhood afternoon vs. a defining life moment |
| **Temperature** | Emotional heat | Recent pain vs. cold old memory |

**Orb lifecycle:** Received from villager → shelved → brought to workbench → operated on → returned/sold/kept → possibly dreamed

### 8.2 The Workbench — The Crafting Core

Four core operations:

#### A) Polish
- **Purpose:** Restore clarity to a faded orb
- **Mini-game:** Gentle circular mouse motion; fill the clarity meter
- **Feel:** Like cleaning an heirloom; satisfying, meditative
- **Audio:** Soft rising hum; a chime when complete
- **Risk:** None — polishing only restores, never damages
- **Auto-Complete:** Always available

#### B) Cleanse
- **Purpose:** Remove cracks by extracting trauma
- **Mini-game:** Pattern-matching trace around cracks, avoiding the warm core
- **Feel:** Like careful surgery; deliberate, tense in a gentle way
- **Audio:** A low friction tone when you nick the core; relief chord on success
- **Risk:** Remove too much → the memory loses its truth
- **Outcomes:** Perfect / Acceptable / Crossed Core / Incomplete
- **Auto-Complete:** Always available

#### C) Weave (Year 2 DLC)
- **Purpose:** Combine two memories into a more powerful, connected one
- **Mini-game:** Tessellation puzzle

#### D) Sever (Year 2 DLC)
- **Purpose:** Split a complex memory into its component parts
- **Mini-game:** Reverse tessellation

### 8.3 The Village Ledger — The Narrative Core

Every villager has a hidden **Memory Map** — 12–20 distinct memories telling their complete life story. Cross-referencing memories reveals the village's hidden truth. Revelation system: enough cross-references → **Revelation Chapters** unlock.

### 8.4 The Moral Compass — The Choice Core

| Choice | Description | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **Erase** | Remove the memory completely | The villager changes. Some heal. Some lose something essential. |
| **Cleanse** | Extract the pain while preserving the love | Some truth survives, some pain goes |
| **Listen** | Keep the memory whole, offer only witness | Nothing changes — but the act of being heard changes everything |
| **Defer** | Return the memory, unsolved | The villager lives with it; comes back changed in a later mission |

No score. No evil ending. The village you inhabit in Year 3 is a direct consequence of every choice.

### 8.5 Memory Tea & The Garden — The Cozy Loop Anchor

| Herb | Property | Tea Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Lavender** | Soothes recent grief | Gerrold becomes calmer, more open |
| **Valerian** | Suppresses anxiety spirals | Unlocks dialogue paths about fear |
| **Sage** | Clarifies old confusion | Helps villagers articulate fragmented memories |
| **Mugwort** | Enhances dream-recall | Memory Dreams become richer and longer |
| **Rosemary** | Sharpens memory precision | Orb operations get wider tolerance windows |

**Garden loop:** Plant seed → ripen over 2–3 days → harvest → brew tea → use as modifier → earn coin → replant.

### P6 — The Memory Wall ✅ COMPLETE

## 10. NARRATIVE ARCHITECTURE & WORLD

### 10.1 The Setting — Saltmere Village

Saltmere is a small English-adjacent autumnal village of ~40 souls. Memories form as glass orbs in moments of strong emotion, detaching from the mind within weeks if unanchored. Left untended, they drift, crack, and dissolve as "the drift."

**The aesthetic:** Warm amber, russet, sage. Mid-October forever. Smoke from chimneys. Bread always baking. Morning dew on the garden. Beeswax and old glass.

### 10.2 Three Layers of Truth
1. **Surface:** A peaceful autumn village with good bread and kind people
2. **Middle:** A village that has survived grief, migration, and a war nobody talks about
3. **Deep:** The Forgotten Year; the sealed memory; the reason Marin left

### 10.3 The Narrator's Voice
The narrator is Marin Vellis, the predecessor. She doesn't know she's the narrator yet. The player won't either for a long time. Her voice: precise, dry, occasionally tender. She has opinions about her former shop.

## 15. MEMORY DREAMS SYSTEM

Memory Dreams are the game's most emotionally resonant feature. When the player sleeps, they enter a dream based on the most emotionally charged memory acquired that day.

Dreams are: fully illustrated · narrated by Marin (unidentified) · branching based on orb treatment · ~30–120 seconds · skippable but always replayable from the Codex.

### Dream Architecture

```
MOST CHARGED ORB selected (color × weight × clarity)
       │
   ├── Polished perfectly? → Vivid dream, full detail
   ├── Polished partially? → Dreamy, partial clarity  
   ├── Cracked and unprocessed? → Fragmented, painful
   └── Echo connection discovered? → Dreams overlap (2+ orbs)
```

### The Mugwort Effect
Mugwort tea: dreams +40% longer, hidden facets surface, Echo connections revealed. Perfect Mugwort dreams can trigger Revelation beats.

## 20. HUMOR & LEVITY SYSTEMS

**Pickle's Rule of Three:** Two comic setups, then one genuine warmth. Players expecting the third joke get something true instead.

**Evening Ledger's Secret Weapon:** On heavy emotional days, one dry comic observation at the very end — after the feeling has settled — releases the tension without undermining it.

## 29. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE

### Tech Stack
- **Engine:** Unity 6000.4.4f1 (Unity 6 LTS)
- **Pipeline:** URP-Mobile (60fps on mid-range Android/Switch)
- **Dialogue:** Yarn Spinner (hand-written only — AI dialogue forbidden)
- **Audio:** Procedural C# synthesis + espeak-ng fallback + Piper TTS

### asmdef Graph (D-035 — No Cycles)

```
Core (state, events, services)
  ├── Memory (orb types, herb SOs, echo data)
  ├── Player (controller, camera, input)
  ├── Save (schema, serialization)
  └── Mission (directors, services, daily loop)
        ├── MiniGames (Polish, Cleanse, Sort, Steep)
        ├── UI → Core only (D-035 no Mission dependency)
        │     ├── Audio (MusicPlayer, MumbleVoice, VoicePlayer)
        │     └── Cutscene (Timeline director)
        └── Dialogue (Yarn, choice router)
```

### The EventBus Pattern
All inter-system communication via typed events on a static EventBus. MissionAudioHooks is a passive observer — zero coupling to Mission directors.

```csharp
EventBus.Publish(new MemoryPolishedEvent(orbId, quality));
EventBus.Publish(new MoralChoiceMadeEvent(choice));
EventBus.Publish(new DayEndedEvent(dayIndex));
```

One click → ~115 seconds → 20+ sub-capstones → fully playable slice. **Idempotent.** Reflection-driven. Safe after every `git pull`.
